PRODUCT CASE STUDY 02

AI Finance Assistant.

Helping young professionals understand their spending, manage budgets and make smarter financial decisions through AI-powered insights.

Explore Case Study → Back to Projects

01. The Problem

Managing money is not always easy.

Young professionals receive monthly income but may struggle to understand their spending patterns, control unnecessary expenses and plan their savings.

Traditional expense tracking tools can show where money was spent, but users may still need help understanding what to do next.

Problem hypothesis: Young professionals need clearer, actionable financial insights rather than just a list of transactions.

02. Target Users

Young Professionals

People managing their first salaries, monthly expenses and savings goals.

Early-Career Employees

People looking for simple ways to understand budgets and spending habits.

03. Proposed Solution

An AI-powered personal finance companion.

The proposed solution combines expense tracking, budget planning and AI-generated explanations to help users understand their financial behaviour.

Smart Spending Insights

Understand monthly spending patterns and categories.

Budget Assistant

Create monthly budgets and monitor spending limits.

AI Recommendations

Receive understandable suggestions based on spending data.

04. MVP Features

  • Manual income and expense entry.
  • Expense categorisation.
  • Monthly budget creation.
  • Spending summary dashboard.
  • AI-powered spending explanations.

05. Success Metrics

  • Weekly active users.
  • Percentage of users creating monthly budgets.
  • Budget tracking completion rate.
  • Repeat usage of financial insights.
  • User satisfaction with recommendations.

06. Future Improvements

  • Automated transaction imports with user permission.
  • Personalised financial insights.
  • Recurring expense detection.
  • Improved financial goal tracking.
