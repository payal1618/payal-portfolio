01. The Problem

Digital distractions interrupt study sessions.

Students may struggle to maintain concentration because of social media, notifications and frequent phone usage.

This is our initial problem hypothesis. We will validate it through user research.

02. Target Users

Initial target audience: college students and students preparing for examinations.

03. Proposed Solution

Smart Focus is a proposed study productivity application that helps users manage distractions through structured focus sessions.

04. Proposed MVP Features

  • Customizable focus timer
  • Distraction management
  • Study session tracking
  • Weekly progress dashboard

05. Success Metrics

  • Completed focus sessions
  • Session completion rate
  • Weekly returning users
  • Average focus session duration
